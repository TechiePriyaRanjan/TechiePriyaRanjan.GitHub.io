As a mentor at the Astrava Hackathon, I led the **AI for Sustainability & Smart City** track, guiding participants in designing innovative, technology-driven solutions to real-world environmental challenges. I supported teams in applying AI concepts to optimize resource utilization, develop climate modeling approaches, and build scalable systems aimed at improving urban sustainability.

Throughout the event, I worked closely with teams to refine problem statements, architect solutions, and overcome technical roadblocks. The focus was on tackling large-scale challenges such as **carbon reduction** and **ecosystem protection**, encouraging participants to think critically about the long-term impact of their solutions while delivering functional prototypes under time constraints.
