Our team developed an AI-powered voice assistant specifically tailored for navigating complex web interfaces. By combining screen reader capabilities with LLM intent recognition, we created a novel way for visually impaired users to interact with rich web applications. The project was awarded 1st place for its innovative approach to UX/UI accessibility.
